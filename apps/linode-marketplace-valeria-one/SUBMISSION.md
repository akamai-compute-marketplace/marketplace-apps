**Description:**
Valeria One is an open-source back office for small businesses that sell through WhatsApp, Instagram
and Messenger. It puts every conversation in one inbox — WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, SMS, email,
Telegram or any system through a signed webhook — and runs what comes after the chat: customers, a
pipeline, booking with no double-booking, quotes signed on the phone, payment links to the business's
own account and automatic reminders. It also builds a simple website. Each business switches on only
what it uses and names things in its own words. It speaks 11 languages, follows 14 countries' tax and
privacy rules, and works without AI. Nightly backups and self-rolling-back updates are included.
